Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Refycon PRO offer?

Refycon PRO offers full renovations of homes and commercial premises, bathroom and kitchen renovations, new builds and villa construction, pools, polished concrete, painting and finishes, and a 24-hour emergency plumbing and electrical service on the Costa del Sol.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across the Costa del Sol: Marbella, San Pedro de Alcántara, Estepona, Benahavís, Manilva, Mijas, Fuengirola, Benalmádena, Torremolinos, Málaga and nearby towns.

How long does a full renovation take?

It depends on the scope of the project. A standard home renovation usually takes 2 to 4 months. We give you a detailed schedule with every quote.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes, we offer free, no-obligation quotes. We visit your property, assess the project and give you a detailed, itemised quote.

Do you have a 24-hour emergency service?

Yes, we offer a 24/7 emergency service for plumbing and electrical problems.
