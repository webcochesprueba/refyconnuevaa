Frequently asked questions

Do you renovate villas in Benahavís?

Yes. We renovate and build villas all over Benahavís, including urbanisations such as La Zagaleta, El Madroñal, La Quinta, Los Arqueros, Monte Mayor and Los Flamingos, with every trade from our own team.

How much does it cost to renovate a villa in Benahavís?

It depends on the size, the scope and the materials. As a 2026 market reference, a full villa renovation with a new layout usually costs €900-1,500/m², and a luxury renovation with premium materials €1,500-2,500/m² or more. After a free site visit we give you a fixed quote with every item itemised.

How long does a full villa renovation take?

It depends on the size and the scope. Before we start we give you a detailed schedule with every phase of the work, and a site manager keeps you informed from start to finish.
