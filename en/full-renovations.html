Frequently asked questions

How much does a full renovation cost on the Costa del Sol?

The cost of a full renovation depends on the square metres, the materials chosen and the complexity of the project. A mid-to-high quality renovation ranges from €600-1,200/m². We provide tailored, no-obligation quotes with every item itemised.

How long does a complete renovation take?

A typical full home renovation (80-120 m²) usually takes 8-16 weeks depending on the scope. Partial renovations such as bathrooms or kitchens can be completed in 2-4 weeks. We always provide a detailed schedule before we start.

What does a full renovation include?

A full renovation includes demolition and waste removal, electrical and plumbing installations, building work, flooring and tiling, interior joinery, painting, sanitaryware and taps, and final finishes. All guaranteed and with first-class materials.
