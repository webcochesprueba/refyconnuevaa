Renovations in Estepona

From the old town to the New Golden Mile

Estepona combines traditional homes in the old town with newer developments in El Paraíso, Cancelada and Valle Romano. Each type of home needs a different renovation: in older houses we usually renew wiring and plumbing and change the layout; in the developments, we update bathrooms, kitchens, terraces and pools. We handle everything with our own team and give you a fixed quote before we start.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do full renovations in Estepona?

Yes. We carry out full renovations of apartments, townhouses and villas all over Estepona: demolition, electrics and plumbing, building work, flooring, joinery, painting and finishes, all with our own team and a single point of contact.

How much does a full renovation cost in Estepona?

It depends on the size, the materials and the condition of the property. A mid-to-high quality renovation is usually between €600 and €1,200 per m². After a free site visit we give you a fixed quote with every item broken down.

Which areas of Estepona do you cover?

The whole municipality: the town centre and old town, El Paraíso, Cancelada, Benamara, Selwo, Valle Romano, Costalita, Atalaya and the New Golden Mile.
