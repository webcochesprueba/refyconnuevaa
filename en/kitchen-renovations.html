Frequently asked questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Marbella?

It depends on the size, whether the layout changes and the units, worktop and appliances chosen. After a free site visit we give you a fixed quote with every item itemised.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

A kitchen renovation is usually completed in 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the scope. Before we start we give you a detailed schedule.

Can the kitchen be opened up to the living room?

In many cases, yes. First we check whether the wall is load-bearing; if it is, we work out the right technical solution. We handle all the work and the rearrangement of the installations.

Want to renovate your kitchen?

Tell us what you have in mind and we will prepare a fixed quote. Free site visit.
