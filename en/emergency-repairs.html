Frequently asked questions

Do you have a 24-hour emergency service?

Yes, our emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays. We have on-call technicians ready to handle any plumbing or electrical emergency on the Costa del Sol.

How quickly can you get to an emergency?

Our average response time is 30-60 minutes depending on the location. We cover the whole Costa del Sol from Málaga to Manilva, with technicians based along the coast to keep waiting times short.

What kind of emergencies do you handle?

All kinds: water leaks, burst pipes, blockages, flooding, short circuits, power cuts, fuse box faults, water heater and boiler breakdowns, and any other plumbing or electrical emergency.
